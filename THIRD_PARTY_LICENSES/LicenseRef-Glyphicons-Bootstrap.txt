The Bootstrap 3.1.1 documentation says that Glyphicons Halflings are made
available free of cost for Bootstrap by their creator, and requests a link
to Glyphicons where possible:
https://github.com/twbs/bootstrap/blob/v3.1.1/docs/components.html

Bootstrap 2.3.2 documents the same special arrangement and optional credit:
https://github.com/twbs/bootstrap/blob/v2.3.2/docs/base-css.html#icons
Its footer separately mentions CC-BY-3.0 for "Glyphicons Free". This inventory
does not assume that statement grants a general CC license for every
Halflings font or sprite.
